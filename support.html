Common questions

How do I turn the keyboard on?
Open Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard… and choose Keycap. Then tap Keycap and turn on Allow Full Access for sounds, haptics, clipboard history and your options.

How do I switch back to another keyboard?
Tap the globe, or touch and hold it to pick from a list.

Why don't my options or clipboard history work?
They need Allow Full Access. Options apply the next time the keyboard opens.

How do I split the keyboard on iPhone Duo?
Turn on "Split keyboard on the big screen" in the Keycap app. Swipe to write is off while split.

Privacy

Keycap makes no network connections, and everything stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
