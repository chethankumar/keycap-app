Keycap Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Keycap is a keyboard for iPhone. It has no servers and makes no network connections, so nothing you type or copy ever leaves your iPhone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and we don't collect any data.

What stays on your iPhone

To work, Keycap keeps a few things in private storage on your device, shared only between the Keycap app and its keyboard:

  • Your settings, such as your theme and key height.
  • Words you keep by undoing an autocorrection, so they aren't corrected again.
  • Where your fingers tend to land, as simple averages for each part of the keyboard, to improve accuracy. Not the text you type.
  • Recently used emoji.
  • Clipboard history, if you leave it on: text you copy, so you can paste it again. Items other apps mark as sensitive, such as those from password managers, are skipped.

Deleting the app deletes all of this. You can turn clipboard history off in the app, and clear it from the keyboard.

Allow Full Access

iOS warns that a keyboard with Full Access could send what you type elsewhere. Keycap can't: it contains no networking at all. It uses Full Access for clipboard history, key sounds and haptics, and so that options you choose in the app reach the keyboard. The keyboard types without it.

Children

Keycap collects no data from anyone, including children.
